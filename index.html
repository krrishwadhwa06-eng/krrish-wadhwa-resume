I study business at IIM Bangalore and work full-time at Agilitas Sports, where I build internal AI tools for ONE8 and Lotto Sport India. Outside sportswear, I am building an AI dashboard that helps a hotel monitor and cut its costs.

I did not start in tech. My first internships were in hotel marketing and at a venture capital fund, and in school I co-founded a clean drinking water nonprofit. In 2026 I joined Agilitas as an intern and spent about eight months moving between strategy, creative, research, operations and analytics. Seeing how much of that work ran on spreadsheets is what pushed me to start building tools.

My goal is to keep working where business and technology meet: understand how a company really runs, then build the systems that make it run better.
